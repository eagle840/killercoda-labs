# Step 5: Timeline Rollbacks, Sharing, and Exporting

Managing history and sharing your work is essential for collaborative development.

### 1. Inspecting History
To see your command and reasoning history, use:
```text
/timeline
```{{copy}}
You can use this timeline to undo specific changes or fork the session into a new branch.

### 2. Exporting and Sharing
* **Export chat**: Export your conversation to Markdown:
  ```text
  /export
  ```{{copy}}
  
* **Share session**: Generate a shareable HTML URL:
  ```text
  /share
  ```{{copy}}
To revoke access later, use `/unshare`.
